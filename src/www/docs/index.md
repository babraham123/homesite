# assorted nerditry

Welcome to my website. It's a place to write down the tech projects I spend too much of my free time on, mostly so future me can remember how they work.

## What's here

**[Homelab](homelab/index.md)**: the docs for my private cloud at home. It's two Proxmox hosts in a closet and a small VPS as the front door, running identity, monitoring, home automation, game streaming and remote access on hardware I own.

**[Blog](blog/index.md)**: longer write-ups, debugging stories, and the occasional opinion.

## Source

Everything is open source. The homelab configs and scripts are at [github.com/babraham123/homelab](https://github.com/babraham123/homelab), and this site is at [github.com/babraham123/homesite](https://github.com/babraham123/homesite). Treat them as inspiration rather than a blueprint: it's one way to build a homelab, tuned to my goals.
